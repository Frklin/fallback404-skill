# not-found: constraints

- Renders inside the site's layout; never repeats its header or footer.
- One `<h1>` with the message, one primary action (the site's button or link), optional links to key pages.
- Any scene is decorative (`data-f404-art`, `aria-hidden="true"`) and at most about 240px tall.
- Works from 320px wide with no horizontal scroll, in light and dark.
- A section's 404 speaks about that section's thing and sends visitors where that section's people go next.
